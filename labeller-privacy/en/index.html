Privacy Policy — Labeller

Last updated: 2026-08-13

1. About Labeller

Labeller: Batch Photo Labels (dev.macsiem.labeller) lets you select a batch of photos from your device, add text boxes, freehand drawing, shapes, and redactions, and then overwrite the originals, save annotated copies, or discard your edits.

Publisher and data controller for the iOS/App Store version: Maciej Siemiński. Data controller for the Android/Google Play version: LICENCEPRO POLSKA Sp. z o.o., ul. Huculska 6, 00-730 Warszawa, Polska (the Play Store version shows advertising, which requires an EU-based advertising data controller). Website: MacSiem.dev · Contact: mail@macsiem.dev

2. Summary in plain language

Photo selection, editing, and final rendering happen locally on your device. Labeller does not require an account or login. If you explicitly share a collection through the optional sync feature, its collection data, labels, and optimized photo files are transferred only to devices you pair, with end-to-end encryption; our relay server cannot read them. Photos outside shared collections are not uploaded by sync. Ads (Unity Ads) run in the free version and use your device's advertising identifier, subject to your consent. See Sections 12–13 for details, and Section 16 for how to delete your data.

3. Local photo processing

Labeller processes the photos you select through the system photo picker or photo-library permission. The selected images and every text box, drawing, shape, or redaction are processed on the device. Photo content is not transmitted unless you explicitly include its collection in optional sync; that limited case is described in Section 12.

4. Overwriting original photos

Labeller can modify your photo library. For each save, you explicitly choose whether to write the annotated image back over the original photo. This action also requires the relevant system permission.

  • On iOS: the edited image is written back to the original photo asset, and the original remains recoverable with the Photos app's own Revert feature.
  • On Android: overwriting replaces the file. Android asks you to confirm the batch through a system dialog before the operation proceeds.

5. Saving copies or discarding edits

You choose the save mode each time. Choosing save as copies creates annotated copies and leaves the original photos untouched. Choosing to discard the edits does not write the edited images to your photo library.

6. Collections stored on the device

Labeller stores collections—batches, local photo copies and their annotations—on your device. A collection is deleted locally when you delete that collection. All local collections are deleted when you delete the app. If you enable sync for a collection, temporary end-to-end encrypted delivery buffers are handled as described in Sections 12, 15, and 16.

7. Photo access on iOS

The iOS version uses the following system photo-library purpose descriptions:

  • NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription — to let you select and work with photos from your library.
  • NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription — to save annotated copies or write an edited image back to the selected asset when you choose that option.

8. Photo access on Android

The Android version uses photo access provided by:

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES — access to images when required for the selected-photo workflow.
  • READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED — access to the photos you choose with the system photo picker.

9. Permissions and identifiers that are blocked

Labeller's manifest explicitly blocks permissions for location, contacts, microphone, and screen overlay. Labeller does not use those capabilities. The advertising identifier is used by the Unity Ads SDK described in Section 13; it is not used for any other purpose.

10. Crash and error diagnostics

Labeller reports crashes and errors to Sentry, using the labeller project in the maciej-sieminski organisation. A diagnostic report may contain the device model, operating-system version, app version, and technical stack traces. These reports are used to identify crashes and errors in the app.

11. What Sentry does not receive

Labeller's Sentry integration scrubs image paths and caption text from diagnostic reports. No photo or caption content is transmitted to Sentry. Image paths and annotation text are not transmitted either.

12. Accounts, servers, and user-to-user features

Labeller does not offer accounts or login. Optional sync lets you explicitly pair another device or person and choose which collections to share. The feature is off by default. For a shared collection, Labeller transfers its name and structure, photo identifiers and dimensions, labels and annotations, and two optimized JPEG variants of each shared photo (a thumbnail and a working-size copy). Local file-system paths and photos outside the selected sharing scope are not sent.

Each device generates its own encryption key pair. Metadata and every photo variant are encrypted end-to-end on the sending device separately for each paired recipient. Our relay service (Cloudflare Workers and R2 object storage) receives only ciphertext and technical delivery metadata such as encrypted-object identifiers, dimensions, sender/recipient device identifiers, timestamps, and byte counts. The relay does not receive the encryption keys and cannot decrypt the content. Encrypted photo buffers are deleted after the receiving device confirms a successful local save, and are also subject to automatic expiry. Turning sharing off stops future transfers but does not remove copies that already reached another device.

13. Advertising and tracking

The free version of Labeller shows ads served by Unity Ads. On iOS, the free version shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt before using the advertising identifier for tracking; if you decline, ads are still shown but are less personalized. On Android, the advertising identifier is used subject to the consent flow shown in the app. Unity Ads may share data with its own advertising partners under Unity's privacy policy. Premium removes ads; when Premium is active, Labeller does not request tracking permission and does not initialize the advertising SDK.

14. In-app purchases

Labeller offers an optional annual Premium subscription, a one-time lifetime Premium purchase, and an optional consumable tip that does not unlock features. Purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play; Labeller does not receive or store your payment-card details. Premium removes ads and raises the product limits described in the app. Store purchase records are used locally to determine and restore your entitlement.

15. Retention and your save choices

Photos remain in your system photo library according to the save choice you make: overwrite the selected originals, save annotated copies while leaving originals untouched, or discard the edits. Collections, received photo copies, and annotations remain on each device until they are deleted there or the app is deleted. When sync is enabled, end-to-end encrypted metadata and photo buffers remain on the relay only for delivery and automatic expiry as described in Section 12.

16. Deleting your data

You can delete everything Labeller stores locally about you at any time: delete a collection in the app to remove it and its annotations from that device, or delete the app to remove all local data at once. Copies already delivered to another device must be deleted on that device. If you have used sync, you can end the collaboration in Settings → Collaborate; this stops future transfers and asks the relay to remove the pairing and its remaining encrypted delivery buffers. Delivered buffers are also deleted after acknowledgement, and undelivered buffers expire automatically. If you would like us to manually confirm deletion of any residual encrypted sync data or technical metadata, contact mail@macsiem.dev and we will act on your request within 30 days.
