Privacy Policy — Labeller

Last updated: 2026-08-03

1. About Labeller

Labeller: Batch Photo Labels (dev.macsiem.labeller) lets you select a batch of photos from your device, add text boxes, freehand drawing, shapes, and redactions, and then overwrite the originals, save annotated copies, or discard your edits.

Publisher and data controller for the iOS/App Store version: Maciej Siemiński. Data controller for the Android/Google Play version: LICENCEPRO POLSKA Sp. z o.o., ul. Huculska 6, 00-730 Warszawa, Polska (the Play Store version shows advertising, which requires an EU-based advertising data controller). Website: MacSiem.dev · Contact: mail@macsiem.dev

2. Summary in plain language

Your photo files never leave your device — selection, editing, and final rendering all happen locally, and photo bytes are never uploaded. Labeller does not require an account or login. An optional, Premium-only sync feature can link your own devices: it sends collection names and labels (not photo files) end-to-end encrypted, so our relay server cannot read them. Ads (Unity Ads) run in the free version and use your device's advertising identifier, subject to your consent. See Sections 12–13 for details, and Section 16 for how to delete your data.

3. Local photo processing

Labeller processes the photos you select through the system photo picker or photo-library permission. The selected images and every text box, drawing, shape, or redaction are processed on the device. No photo content is uploaded or transmitted.

4. Overwriting original photos

Labeller can modify your photo library. For each save, you explicitly choose whether to write the annotated image back over the original photo. This action also requires the relevant system permission.

  • On iOS: the edited image is written back to the original photo asset, and the original remains recoverable with the Photos app's own Revert feature.
  • On Android: overwriting replaces the file. Android asks you to confirm the batch through a system dialog before the operation proceeds.

5. Saving copies or discarding edits

You choose the save mode each time. Choosing save as copies creates annotated copies and leaves the original photos untouched. Choosing to discard the edits does not write the edited images to your photo library.

6. Collections stored on the device

Labeller stores collections—batches and their annotations—locally on your device. A collection is deleted when you delete that collection. All collections are deleted when you delete the app.

7. Photo access on iOS

The iOS version uses the following system photo-library purpose descriptions:

  • NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription — to let you select and work with photos from your library.
  • NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription — to save annotated copies or write an edited image back to the selected asset when you choose that option.

8. Photo access on Android

The Android version uses photo access provided by:

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES — access to images when required for the selected-photo workflow.
  • READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED — access to the photos you choose with the system photo picker.

9. Permissions and identifiers that are blocked

Labeller's manifest explicitly blocks permissions for location, contacts, microphone, and screen overlay. Labeller does not use those capabilities. The advertising identifier is used by the Unity Ads SDK described in Section 13; it is not used for any other purpose.

10. Crash and error diagnostics

Labeller reports crashes and errors to Sentry, using the labeller project in the maciej-sieminski organisation. A diagnostic report may contain the device model, operating-system version, app version, and technical stack traces. These reports are used to identify crashes and errors in the app.

11. What Sentry does not receive

Labeller's Sentry integration scrubs image paths and caption text from diagnostic reports. No photo or caption content is transmitted to Sentry. Image paths and annotation text are not transmitted either.

12. Accounts, servers, and user-to-user features

Labeller does not offer accounts or login. An optional Premium feature, sync, lets you link your own devices to keep collection names and labels in step between them. Sync is off by default and only sends collection and label metadata — never photo files or paths — and only between devices you have paired yourself. Each device generates its own encryption key pair; data is end-to-end encrypted before it leaves the device, and our relay server (Cloudflare Workers/R2) stores the encrypted buffer only long enough to deliver it, then deletes it. The relay cannot decrypt your data. Sync does not create any connection or data sharing between different users' accounts — it only links devices you control.

13. Advertising and tracking

The free version of Labeller shows ads served by Unity Ads. On iOS, Labeller shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt before using the advertising identifier for tracking; if you decline, ads are still shown but are less personalized. On Android, the advertising identifier is used subject to the consent flow shown in the app. Unity Ads may share data with its own advertising partners under Unity's privacy policy. Premium removes ads.

14. In-app purchases

The current version of Labeller has no active in-app purchases.

15. Retention and your save choices

Photos remain in your system photo library according to the save choice you make: overwrite the selected originals, save annotated copies while leaving originals untouched, or discard the edits. Collections and their annotations remain only on the device until you delete the collection or delete the app.

16. Deleting your data

You can delete everything Labeller stores about you at any time: delete a collection in the app to remove it and its annotations from your device, or delete the app to remove all local data at once. If you have used sync, you can disconnect pairing in Settings → Sync, which deletes your paired devices' encrypted data from the relay server; because relay data also expires automatically a short time after each sync, no separate request is normally needed. If you would like us to manually confirm deletion of any residual sync metadata, contact mail@macsiem.dev and we will act on your request within 30 days.
