Privacy Policy — Riffle

Last updated: 2026-08-12

By default Riffle runs entirely on your device and needs no server. Your photos and ratings only leave the device with optional pairing with another person (section 4) — only then does some data pass through a relay over an encrypted channel. The free version additionally shows ads (Unity Ads) that use your device's advertising identifier subject to your consent (section 5); Premium removes ads.

2. What data we process

Riffle helps you sift photo options: you add photos, mark them as “no”, “maybe” or “yes”, and group them into collections.

The app stores on your device: the photos you picked, your ratings, collection names, and settings (theme, language, haptics).

By default this data is never uploaded — there is no account, no sign-in and no cloud backup. The only exception is optional pairing with another person, described in section 4.

3. Photo access

The app uses the system photo picker. It only sees the photos you select there — it has no access to your full library. Selected photos are copied into the app's private storage.

4. Pairing and sync (optional)

Riffle lets you optionally pair two devices so you can rate photos together with another person. The feature is off by default and only starts once you enable it and use a pairing code.

When you use it:

  • A nickname you choose, a random device identifier and public keys are sent to the relay (an intermediary server at riffle-pair.maciek-sieminski.workers.dev). They are used solely to connect your two devices. The nickname is visible to the relay.
  • Photos and ratings shared between paired devices are end-to-end encrypted on your device before they leave it. The relay cannot read their contents — it only holds them briefly, until the other device downloads them, after which they are removed.
  • The relay does not store your photo library or ratings — only what is necessary to route data between the two paired devices.

Unpairing removes the pair's data from the relay. If you do not use pairing, none of the above data leaves your device.

5. Advertising and tracking

The free version of Riffle shows ads served by Unity Ads — the only ad network used in the app. To select, deliver and measure ads, the Unity Ads SDK collects device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (advertising ID). On iOS, Riffle shows the system App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier is used for tracking; if you decline, ads are still shown but are less personalized. On Android, the advertising identifier (AD_ID) is used subject to the consent prompt shown in the app — you can choose non-personalized ads there and change that choice at any time in Settings. Unity Ads may share data with its own advertising partners under Unity's privacy policy. Premium removes ads.

6. Analytics and crash reporting

The app collects no usage analytics. If crash reporting is enabled in a given build, only a technical error report is sent (device model, OS version, app version, stack trace). Reports contain none of your photos, ratings or collection names.

7. Deleting your data

Deleting a collection in the app permanently removes its photos and ratings. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device. If you used pairing, unpairing removes the pair's data from the relay; otherwise we hold no copy of your content.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

9. Changes

If future versions add features that require transmitting data, this policy will be updated before such a feature ships.
